# ART-384: guest LAB checkout and admission

Owner contract: [ART-384](https://linear.app/art-gurianov/issue/ART-384),
2026-10-17 decision; implemented on top of ART-373 and ART-199.

## Identity and checkout

LAB uses the existing nullable-auth customer model. Email is the only required
contact; an account is optional. An unverified supplied email does **not** grant
customer-wide access. A 32-byte browser-generated capability, persisted in
session storage before the first request, names one server-generated order.
Commerce stores its SHA-256 digest. Repeating start after a lost answer returns
that same order; changing email under that capability is refused.

The alternatives were mandatory magic-link login, which contradicts the owner
decision, and an email-wide guest session, which would expose another buyer's
orders before mailbox verification. The order capability keeps the established
customer/binding model while limiting the guest's authority.

Preview and explicit confirmation use ART-373's durable intent. Keys are scoped
to the guest order. Offer kind is checked before any checkout reconciliation;
guests cannot purchase LMS products. Confirmation verifies that the quote names
this exact order. Existing signed-in LMS authentication is preserved.

The LAB Stage B legal release and qualified fiscal policy remain prerequisites.
The browser shows the frozen occurrence, venue, price and current legal links.
Three unchecked choices cover personal-data processing, LAB offer/refund terms
and receipt contact. Confirmation records release version/hash and these choices
in the order capability's evidence within the order/attempt transaction. This is
order-scoped evidence with an unverified email, never authenticated account or
marketing consent. The frozen payment request carries the normalized receipt
email; resolution payloads contain no buyer identity.

## Tickets, recovery and entry

Only accepted payment of the frozen amount issues a ticket, once per order line.
Pending, unknown, declined and amount-mismatched payments issue none. The guest
can reconcile their own order without starting another payment.

The QR is a separate HMAC-derived 256-bit entry capability. Its digest is stored
on first ticket read. It gives no order/refund authority. The browser renders the
QR locally from a PNG generated by Commerce, with no external QR service.
Static `/ticket` is noindex and no-referrer. Links use fragments; API capabilities
travel in Authorization or POST bodies, with no-store responses.

Email recovery accepts email alone (up to 20 most recent purchased orders in one
message) or an optional exact order number. Absent, mismatched, suppressed,
throttled and failed-delivery requests receive the same acknowledgement. SQLite
limits requests to three per email per 15-minute window and 100 globally.
Transactional delivery reuses the configured NotiSend/HTTP transport and the
shared erasure SEND fence. It does not enable marketing or resend automatically
after an ambiguous delivery. An immediately paid checkout attempts an initial
ticket email once; pending-payment buyers can read back and request recovery.

Recovery links expire after 15 minutes. Loading a link does not replace anything:
an explicit POST consumes it, changes the order capability and invalidates all
old QR/recovery credentials. A lost confirmation answer can replay the same
replacement capability. A verified account owning the same customer can also
reissue. Neither operation changes the ticket's identity or use record.

Entry requires an active Control Room session and an explicit occurrence plus
QR. A SQLite immediate transaction and compare-and-set commit `used_at/used_by`
once; repeated/wrong-event/void scans refuse. Database triggers prohibit clearing
or rewriting a use record and deleting a used ticket. Recovery, account binding,
payment reconciliation and refund cannot restore entry. The LAB Control Room
screen accepts a scanner's text and requires explicit operator confirmation.

## Refunds, restore and boundaries

Guests can create/read refund cases for their exact order. Existing separate
operator decision, encrypted submission envelope and accepted refund readback
govern execution. A full refund makes entry void; a late PAID projection cannot
revive a refunded order. Use is retained as evidence, with no invented automatic
refund eligibility rule.

Migration 0032 adds guest access, ticket, recovery and hashed rate-limit stores.
Erasure removes unpurchased guest access and cascades recovery credentials;
financial orders/tickets retain the existing financial-review refusal. An
isolated restore revokes all guest/recovery/QR credentials, preserving use records.
Offline retention preserves guest-access facts rather than inventing retention
periods or deleting a customer through a new FK. Exact schema admission includes
0032 while retaining previously reviewed backup schemas.

No deploy, production migration, real payment/refund/email, sales activation or
F9 is authorized. Tests and browser evidence use an isolated mock rail and mail
sink. The live vNext rail/arrival UI integration and closed cross-service E2E
remain the separate C3/D/E1 issues (C3, ART-379/ART-380); this change does not retrofit
the obsolete RC.2 attribution handoff. LAB legal/fiscal/refund acceptance remains
separate from LMS and ART-243 production acceptance.
