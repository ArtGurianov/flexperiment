Owner direction: finish technical preparation autonomously after the docs-only PR237; keep final legal approval, actual1₽ charge and public activation separate. Reuse existing issues and the closed LMS, with ART-338 finally Done.

Use an explicit `art39-test-course-rub1-v1` profile on the current staging/canary. It binds course/offer `test-course` / `course:test-course`, the SHA-256 of one normalized verified buyer email,100kopecks, protected Kinescope, disabled marketing, the production Refref API/checkout/readiness origins and an exact Refref source SHA. It also pins the final Stage B legal version and canonical manifest hash; the draft packet hash is not that runtime hash. Missing/malformed inputs fail closed. Defaults stay disabled. Rejected alternatives: a bare PAYMENT_MODE flip breaks readiness/backup and lacks scope; a second Refref-canary bank setup duplicates the existing production connection; a direct provider payment skips the merchant checkout/fulfillment contract.

Every new preview and confirm re-reads the live offer and checks kind, access model, course, offer, price, sale mode and the sole allowlisted buyer. Catalog writes cannot open another paid offer or a PUBLIC/LAB/bundle sale through this profile. Promo input/upstream discounts and different fiscal values refuse before a payment is created. One historical order for this experiment prevents another charge, including after access ends; exact idempotent retries reconcile the original attempt. The final duplicate-order check is under SQLite's write transaction.

Preview exposes the pinned paid documents and verified receipt email without recording acceptance. A single unchecked checkbox lets the buyer accept the purchase/refund terms and acknowledge the data/receipt disclosure. Confirm validates explicit acceptance, version, manifest hash and receipt email. Its authenticated buyer comes from the session; body customer identifiers are ignored. The durable acceptance event is inserted in the same transaction as the order and links quote, canonical legal hash/version, contact hash and authenticated source. Replays do not fabricate another acceptance; no schema migration or manual entitlement is needed. Existing account/PD/marketing actions remain separate.

Readiness probes the actual production Refref dependency and refuses another source; Platform requires matching Commerce source, refref mode, profile identity and healthy dependency. The host runtime/backup observer accepts refref only with an independently installed root-owned0600 `paid-test.json` and matching per-role env. The observer file cannot change env/images, qualify a policy or grant erasure admission. No erasure/lineage/image/source guard changes. Initial fast-cut prepare remains disabled-only.

Validation covers negative scope and stale-price/legal/contact cases before upstream create, one order/acceptance on retries, real HTTP wiring, the buyer checkbox and Python observer contract, alongside existing checkout/refund/foundation and Platform tests. CI/build/install/provider receipts stay distinct.
